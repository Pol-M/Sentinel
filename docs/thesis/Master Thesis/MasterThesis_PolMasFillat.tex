\chapter*{Abstract}
%\emph{Draft abstract -- to be revised as the project progresses.}

Sentinel is a mechatronic system designed to function as an automated
electronic climbing belay device. Unlike conventional belay devices, which
still require a human operator to feed rope and arrest falls, Sentinel aims
to autonomously manage rope slack during a climber's ascent and to trigger
a fast-acting mechanical brake upon detecting a fall. This thesis documents 
the design, implementation, and evaluation of a functional prototype, together
with an analysis of the regulatory and safety context relevant to automated belay devices.
